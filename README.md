# The Wizard's Son

**The Wizard's Son** is a single-player, top-down pixel-art roguelike dungeon crawler developed by Oozma Kappa.

## About the Game
Play as Sisyphus, son of Aeolus, as he battles through a dungeon to escape his father's imprisonment. Defeat enemies, earn experience, upgrade your abilities, and take your chances with randomized rewards. If you die, the dungeon resets, and your journey begins again!

## Features
- Top-down dungeon combat with multiple enemy types
- Experience-based leveling and upgrades
- Randomized blessings and curses through the Wheel of Persistence
- Gold, weapons, armor, and other rewards
- Challenging boss encounters and replayable gameplay
